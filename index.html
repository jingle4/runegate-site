Runegate

A roguelite tile-matching RPG for iPhone and iPad.

Coming soon to the App Store

Chain tiles to swing your weapon, raise armor, drink potions and gather coins. Fight your way down an endless gate: bosses, guardians and a Nemesis wait below.

  • Three classesWarrior, Mage and Ranger, each with their own abilities, room and emblem.
  • Endless runsOne board, a boss every few rounds, and a Store after every kill.
  • Build your runItems, affixes and unlocks that carry over from attempt to attempt.
  • Three tiersNormal, Hard and Nightmare, each tuned with its own boss curve.
  • Pixel artEvery tile, room and menu drawn in pixel art.
  • PrivateNo accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Requires iOS 26.4 or later.
